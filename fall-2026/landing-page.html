The concept

How it works

Both rosters play tournaments. It's one program with one practice plan — the split only exists on game weekends.

Split into two rosters

Navy and Gray, about 11 players each. Everyone practices together — two field practices a week plus four cage sessions a month.

Alternate weekends

Navy plays a tournament one week, Gray the next. Each roster sees roughly two tournaments a month without burning out families every weekend.

Fill the gaps

Short a catcher? Missing an arm? Players from the other roster fill in as needed — it's one team, so nobody plays short-handed.
