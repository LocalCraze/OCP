## Fall 2026 concept (proposed)

One team, two rosters — a **Navy** roster and a **Gray** roster of ~11 players
each (22 total). Both rosters play tournaments, alternating weekends: Navy
plays one week, Gray the next, and players fill in gaps on the other roster
as needed. Coaches run practices themselves, so coaches' kids are exempt
from the monthly practice fee.
